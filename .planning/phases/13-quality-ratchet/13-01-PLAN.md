## Sequence
PR-A and PR-B-time develop in parallel in two lane worktrees; PR-B-coverage starts when PR-A merges; PR-B-exports when PR-B-time merges; PR-C-props when PR-B-coverage merges; PR-C-mutation last. The orchestrator verifies each pull request (typecheck, the touched tests, lint on the changed files, `check:docs`, `check:gates`, `test:scripts`; the full suite and the full lint once before each push that changes `src` or tests), pushes, and takes it through the review loop to 5 of 5 and the merge. CI (the `verify` matrix on the self-hosted runner) is the authority for the full gates.

## Acceptance
The five items of `13-CONTEXT.md`, plus: no test is deleted or skipped to get green, no suppression directive is added, no coverage threshold is lowered, and every dependency or workflow change carries its justification or D6 citation.

## Risks and stop conditions
See `13-CONTEXT.md`. Additionally: if a pull request needs the CI numbers it cannot get from this host, it lands report-only and the follow-up pull request sets the floors.
