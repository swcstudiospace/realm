The 2026-11-11 exception permits only provisional, offline and unmerged 20-01 execution after independent review of the revised plan; its provisional plan completion does not imply joined readiness or Phase 20 completion, or permit starting 20-02. The parent establishes that Phase 13, Phase 19, full live production acceptance and all of 20-03 have passed on the applicable integrated revision before 20-02 begins. D6 authorizes necessary protected workflow/release-script changes and is cited in applicable PR bodies, but does not replace actual human review. The parent or specifically authorized operator alone may use the approved ship/test-release route after Task 1. Before any privileged Pages execution or release acceptance, the parent must identify and obtain evidence of an independently enforced control outside the selected workflow revision preventing untrusted revisions from obtaining Pages/OIDC deployment authority. Record the actual policy or enforcement mechanism, scope and trusted revision restriction, plus a real denial or equivalent authoritative verification. Neither existing records nor this plan establish such a control. If unavailable, stop and report the missing prerequisite; do not change repository or environment settings, treat human review or D6 as enforcement, or claim an in-workflow self-check closes this boundary.

## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Risk |
| --- | --- |
| Untrusted workflow revision → privileged Pages deployment | Self-checks may be omitted by that revision. |
| Build artifact → publish and attestation | Incorrect or mismatched artifacts may be published. |

| Shared self-hosted runner → successive jobs | Separate job tokens do not prove OS-user or machine isolation. |
